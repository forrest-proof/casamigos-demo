One truck, three ways to run it

Tier 1 · Flagship

Festival / Large Event

500–1,000 people per event

The big build: two Broncos, a 20′×20′ truss, a separate three-tap bar, a taco cart and a roaming photographer. Three-day build.

Tier 2 · Regional events

Lighter build, same crew

~500 people per event

A 20′×10′ truss and a lighter bar. It can also run with no truss for tighter spaces.

Tier 3 · Retail accounts

Leanest cut

50–250 people per half-day

One truck, a compact bar, no truss and no permit. Runs in stores, suites and rooftops, on or off premise.
